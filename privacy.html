Privacy Policy
Last updated: 26 August 2026

At Ambika Flowers ("we", "us", "our"), your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use ambikaflowers.in (the "Website"), how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the Website you consent to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

  • Details you provide: your name, phone number, email address, delivery and billing address, recipient details, greeting/gift messages, and any information you enter while creating an account or placing an order.
  • Order & transaction details: the products ordered, amount, delivery slot, and order status.
  • Technical data: basic information such as your browser type and actions on the Website, used to keep the site working and improve your experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process, prepare, and deliver your orders.
  • To contact you about your order, delivery, or customer-support requests.
  • To send order confirmations, updates, and (if you opt in) offers and reminders.
  • To prevent fraud and keep the Website and our customers safe.
  • To comply with legal and accounting requirements.

3. Payment Information

Online payments are processed by secure, PCI-DSS compliant third-party payment gateways (such as Razorpay). We do not collect or store your card number, CVV, UPI PIN or net-banking passwords on our servers. All payment details are entered directly on the payment gateway over an encrypted connection.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

The Website uses your browser's local storage and similar technologies to remember items in your cart, keep you signed in, and improve your experience. You can clear this data anytime from your browser settings.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only where necessary: with delivery staff to fulfil your order, with payment gateways to process payments, or where required by law. Any partners who handle your data are expected to protect it.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorised access, loss or misuse. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us using the details below. You may also opt out of promotional messages at any time.

8. Children's Privacy

The Website is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page with the "Last updated" date.
